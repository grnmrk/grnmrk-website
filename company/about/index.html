Our Mission

Make partnerships easier to work with across the company.

Most companies know partnerships matter. What they underestimate is how much ownership, coordination, and operating discipline it takes to turn partner opportunities into outcomes.

Work stalls when priorities are unclear, handoffs are manual, and next steps live in scattered threads. Every one of those failure modes shows up as missed deals, delayed decisions, and tired teams.

Grnmrk exists to close that gap. We design the partnerships function and help run it: strategy, ownership, partner ops, and progress accountability. Specialist cloud modules are available when needed. They are not the whole story.

Our Story

Why we started this.

"The partner opportunity was not the problem. It was everything around it."

Grnmrk was founded by practitioners who spent years inside technology companies running partnerships work: priorities, ownership, coordination, and the execution layer that turns opportunities into outcomes.

What became clear over time was that the companies doing this well were not necessarily the ones with the best products. They were the ones with someone accountable for the partnerships function: clear next steps, usable workflows, and progress the rest of the company could trust.

Most companies do not have that person. They have a founder or sales leader wearing the partnerships hat between other priorities. Grnmrk exists to be that ownership: embedded, practical, and commercially aware.

How We Work

Four things we do not compromise on.

Specificity Over Speed

We would rather spend an extra day understanding your deal history and territory than hand you a generic co-sell framework that does not fit. Every engagement is scoped around your situation.

Outcomes Over Outputs

We do not measure success by documents delivered or calls attended. We measure it by clearer ownership, completed next steps, and measurable partnership outcomes.

Transparency Over Comfort

If ownership is unclear or the operating model will not hold, we will say so. Honest assessment upfront saves both sides from a frustrating engagement.

Depth Over Breadth

We work with a limited number of clients at any given time. That is deliberate. We would rather be genuinely embedded in a few partnerships functions than spread thin across twenty.
