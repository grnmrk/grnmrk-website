Our Mission
GG
Close the gap between
AWS partnership
and AWS revenue.

Most companies that join the AWS Partner Network have the right instinct — they know the AWS relationship matters, they know Marketplace is growing, they know co-sell is where enterprise deals get done. What they underestimate is how much operational infrastructure it takes to make any of it work.

ACE pipeline hygiene. PDM relationship cadence. MAP funding applications. FTR submissions. ISV Accelerate enrollment. Tier requirement tracking. Certification roadmaps. Every one of these has a failure mode — and most companies discover them the hard way, usually when a big deal is on the table and the co-sell motion falls apart.

Grnmrk exists to close that gap. We handle the operational layer that most alliances teams don't have the bandwidth to run correctly, and the strategic layer that most external consultants don't have the depth to advise on. The result: clients who show up to every AWS interaction as a credible, investment-worthy partner — not as a name in the catalog.

Our Story

Why We
Started This.

"The AWS partnership wasn't the problem. It was everything around it."

Grnmrk Group was founded by practitioners who spent years inside technology companies managing AWS partnerships — building ACE pipelines from scratch, running MAP funding cycles, navigating FTR submissions, and trying to get AWS field reps to actually route deals.

What became clear over time was that the companies doing this well weren't necessarily the ones with the best products. They were the ones with someone in the building who understood the program — how the PDM relationship actually works, what gets a funding request approved, what makes an AWS field rep want to attach you to their deal.

Most ISVs don't have that person. They have a founder or a sales leader wearing the alliances hat between other priorities, a PDM they see once a quarter, and a funding opportunity they're probably leaving on the table. Grnmrk exists to be that person — embedded, accountable, and working the AWS relationship every day.

Not a Generalist.
Not a Staffing Firm.

There's no shortage of consultants who claim to do channel strategy. Here's what separates Grnmrk.

01
AWS Only. Nothing Else.

We don't do Azure. We don't do GCP. We don't do general channel strategy or alliances theory. Every client engagement, every tool, every process we've built is designed around the AWS partner ecosystem specifically. That focus is the reason our clients don't have to educate us.

02
Operators, Not Advisors

Most consultants hand you a strategy deck and move on. We run the play. ACE submissions, funding applications, Partner Central management, PDM prep — we do the work. Our clients measure us on outcomes, not deliverables.

03
Embedded, Not Parachuted

We work alongside your team, not above them. We learn your product, your territory, your deal history. We become the alliances function your internal team actually wants to work with — not a vendor they have to manage.

04
No Bloated Retainers

Our managed services start at $2,500/month and our consulting is scoped and priced by project. We don't build in padding. We don't upsell you into more than you need. The scope is honest upfront.

05
Field Intelligence, Not Theory

06
We Tell You If You're Not Ready

We'd rather send you to a different service than take your money for work that won't move the needle. Our discovery call is free and honest. That's a deliberate policy, not a sales tactic.

How We Work

Four Things
We Don't
Compromise On.

Specificity Over Speed

We'd rather spend an extra day understanding your deal history and territory than hand you a generic co-sell framework that doesn't fit. Every engagement is scoped around your actual situation.

Outcomes Over Outputs

We don't measure success by documents delivered or calls attended. We measure it by MAP funding captured, tier requirements closed, FTR approvals granted, and deals with AWS involvement attached.
