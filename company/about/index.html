Our Mission

Close the gap between AWS partnership and AWS revenue.

Most companies that join the AWS Partner Network have the right instinct: they know the AWS relationship matters, they know Marketplace is growing, they know co-sell is where enterprise deals get done. What they underestimate is how much operational infrastructure it takes to make any of it work.

ACE pipeline hygiene. PDM relationship cadence. MAP funding applications. FTR submissions. SaaS Co-sell Benefit enrollment. Tier requirement tracking. Certification roadmaps. Every one of these has a failure mode, and most companies discover them the hard way, usually when a big deal is on the table and the co-sell motion falls apart.

Grnmrk exists to close that gap. We handle the operational layer that most alliances teams do not have the bandwidth to run correctly, and the strategic layer that most external consultants do not have the depth to advise on. The result: clients who show up to every AWS interaction as a credible, investment-worthy partner.

Our Story

Why we started this.

"The AWS partnership was not the problem. It was everything around it."

Grnmrk Group was founded by practitioners who spent years inside technology companies managing AWS partnerships: building ACE pipelines from scratch, running MAP funding cycles, navigating FTR submissions, and trying to get AWS field reps to route deals.

What became clear over time was that the companies doing this well were not necessarily the ones with the best products. They were the ones with someone in the building who understood the program: how the PDM relationship works, what gets a funding request approved, what makes an AWS field rep want to attach you to their deal.

Most ISVs do not have that person. They have a founder or a sales leader wearing the alliances hat between other priorities, a PDM they see once a quarter, and a funding opportunity they are probably leaving on the table. Grnmrk exists to be that person: embedded, accountable, and working the AWS relationship every day.

How We're Different

Not a generalist. Not a staffing firm.

There is no shortage of consultants who claim to do channel strategy. Here is what separates Grnmrk.

01
AWS Only. Nothing Else.

We do not do Azure. We do not do GCP. We do not do general channel strategy or alliances theory. Every client engagement, every tool, every process we have built is designed around the AWS partner ecosystem specifically. That focus is the reason our clients do not have to educate us.

02
Operators, Not Advisors

Most consultants hand you a strategy deck and move on. We run the play. ACE submissions, funding applications, Partner Central management, PDM prep: we do the work. Our clients measure us on outcomes, not deliverables.

03
Embedded, Not Parachuted

We work alongside your team, not above them. We learn your product, your territory, your deal history. We become the alliances function your internal team wants to work with: not a vendor they have to manage.

04
No Bloated Retainers

Our managed services start at $2,500/month and our consulting is scoped and priced by project. We do not build in padding. We do not upsell you into more than you need. The scope is honest upfront.

05
Field Intelligence, Not Theory

We know how AWS field reps think because we have worked with them. We know what gets a PDM to prioritize you because we have watched what does not. That field intelligence shapes every recommendation we make.

06
We Tell You If You're Not Ready

We would rather send you to a different service than take your money for work that will not move the needle. Our discovery call is free and honest. That is a deliberate policy, not a sales tactic.

How We Work

Four things we do not compromise on.

Specificity Over Speed

We would rather spend an extra day understanding your deal history and territory than hand you a generic co-sell framework that does not fit. Every engagement is scoped around your situation.

Outcomes Over Outputs

We do not measure success by documents delivered or calls attended. We measure it by MAP funding captured, tier requirements closed, FTR approvals granted, and deals with AWS involvement attached.

Transparency Over Comfort

If your ACE pipeline is a mess, we will tell you. If you are not ready for FTR, we will say that too. Honest assessment upfront saves both sides from a frustrating engagement.

Depth Over Breadth

We work with a limited number of clients at any given time. That is deliberate. We would rather be genuinely embedded in a few AWS partnerships than spread thin across twenty.
