The Reality

AWS Doesn't Hold
Your Hand at the Start.

The AWS Partner Network is one of the most powerful go-to-market engines in enterprise software. But for partners just joining, it can feel like you've been handed a key to a building with no map. Partner Central is complex, the requirements aren't obvious, and the PDM who's supposed to help you is managing 100+ other partners.

Most early-stage partners spend their first 12 months making avoidable mistakes — choosing the wrong partner path, skipping the ACE setup, missing early funding opportunities, and building toward the wrong tier requirements. By the time they figure it out, they've lost a year.

  • No PDM assigned — or one who's too stretched to actually help
  • Partner Central is confusing and the right next step isn't obvious
  • Tier requirements aren't clearly explained — you're guessing at what matters
  • ACE pipeline sits empty because no one told you how to use it
  • Funding programs like MAP and MDF are invisible until it's too late to apply
  • No certification roadmap — teams are getting random certs instead of the right ones
  • FTR and ISV Accelerate are on the horizon but no one's explained the path

"The first year in the AWS partner ecosystem sets the trajectory for everything that follows. Partners who get the foundation right in year one are the ones climbing tiers and capturing funding in year two. The ones who wing it spend years trying to undo what they got wrong."

What a real PDM does

A PDM is your advocate inside AWS. They explain how the programs actually work, help you navigate Partner Central, brief you on funding opportunities, guide your tier advancement, and introduce you to the right field teams when you're ready to co-sell. Most early-stage partners don't get this. We give it to you.

Six core areas of support, delivered monthly. No fluff, no generic advice — everything is specific to your partner profile, your tier, and your goals.

01
Partner Central Navigation & Setup

We make sure your Partner Central account is set up correctly from the start — right partner path, accurate company profile, solutions listed, and no gaps that will cause problems later. Most partners get this wrong and don't find out until they apply for something.

02
Tier Roadmap & Requirement Tracking

We audit your current standing against your target tier, build a written roadmap to get there, and track your progress monthly. You'll always know exactly where you are and what the next action is — no guessing at what AWS is evaluating.

03
ACE Pipeline Setup & First Submissions

We set up your ACE pipeline correctly, walk you through the submission process, and help you get your first qualified opportunities in the system. An active ACE pipeline from day one changes how AWS sees you immediately.

04
Funding Program Awareness & Early Applications

We identify which MDF, MAP, and other funding programs you're eligible for as an early-stage partner and flag application windows before they close. Most early-stage partners don't know these programs exist until it's too late.

05
Certification Roadmap

We build a certification plan aligned to your tier goals and team makeup — so your people are getting the right AWS certifications, not random ones. Certification drift is one of the most common reasons partners stall at Select.

06
Monthly Check-in & Progress Review

A dedicated monthly call with a Grnmrk advisor — reviewing your tier progress, ACE pipeline, upcoming funding windows, certification status, and any Partner Central issues. The meeting your PDM should be having with you but probably isn't.
