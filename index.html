How engagements work

Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute call.

From there, most engagements fall into one of three shapes, and many clients move between them as their program matures.

Phase 01
Initial Assessment

A full audit of your current AWS partner standing, delivered as a written roadmap you can execute with or without us.

Phase 02
Program Buildout

Focused projects that build the program infrastructure — FTR, Marketplace, co-sell playbooks, and tier acceleration plans.

Phase 03
Ongoing Operations

Monthly retainer engagements where we run the program for you, including fractional senior leadership when the business needs it.
