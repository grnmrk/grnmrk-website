Why this matters

AWS partnerships are high-maintenance by design

The AWS Partner Network is one of the most valuable growth channels available to software and services businesses. Billions of dollars flow through Marketplace each year, co-sell produces real enterprise pipeline, and funding programs like MAP and MDF offset significant cost for the partners who know how to access them.

But the programs that produce those results take consistent, ongoing work. ACE pipeline needs daily hygiene. Funding has narrow application windows. PDM relationships cool off when nobody is tending to them. Marketplace listings need active optimization. Tier requirements shift. FTR standards get updated.

Most partner teams don't have the bandwidth to run all of this well, especially if the AWS relationship is one of several cloud partnerships they're managing. The result isn't failure; it's value left on the table. Funding that never gets applied for. Co-sell deals that never get attributed. Marketplace listings that never transact.

  • 01

    ACE pipeline needs daily hygiene to stay clean, progressing, and properly attributed in Partner Central.

  • 02

    Funding programs like MAP, MDF, POC credits, and SCA have strict timing windows and eligibility rules most teams learn about too late.

  • 03

    AWS Marketplace rewards active listing management, private offer strategy, and a real CPPO motion. Passive listings rarely transact.

  • 04

    PDM and field relationships require a consistent cadence. Once they go cold, co-sell opportunities stop getting routed.

  • 05

    Tier advancement and FTR involve shifting requirements that are easy to misread without someone tracking them weekly.

What we do after that depends entirely on where you are and what you need. Most engagements fall into one of three shapes, and many clients move between them as their program matures.
