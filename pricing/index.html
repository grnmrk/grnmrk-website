Questions

Common questions before getting started.

Can AWS partner funding offset the cost?
+

In many cases, yes. Depending on your partner status and the engagement type, programs like MAP, MDF, and POC credits can offset a meaningful portion of fees. We identify those opportunities during the discovery call and factor them into the scope.

Can I run multiple services at the same time?
+

Yes. Most clients run a managed services program alongside project-based consulting or a revenue program. The Fractional Alliances Leader typically layers on top of a Scale-tier managed retainer. We'll tell you what makes sense for your program on the discovery call.

How is the Fractional Alliances Leader scoped?
+

It depends on the program's complexity and the level of weekly involvement needed. We walk through both on the discovery call and confirm pricing before any work begins.

What does the discovery call cover?
+

Thirty minutes. We review your current AWS partner standing — tier, ACE pipeline, funding history, Marketplace status — and what you're trying to accomplish. From there we scope the engagement and confirm pricing before you commit to anything.

Who owns the deliverables?
+

You do. Every playbook, submission document, and process we build belongs to your company.
