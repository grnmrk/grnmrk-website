Common questions

Questions we hear before every engagement.

Are there long-term contracts?
+

No. Managed services are month-to-month. Consulting engagements are scoped per project. You can pause, stop, or change scope at any time.

Can I combine services?
+

Yes. Most clients combine a managed services retainer with project-based consulting or revenue generation programs as needs arise. The Fractional Alliances Leader role typically sits on top of the managed retainer and Marketplace motion.

Do you offer a discovery call before committing?
+

Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute strategy call. We review your current AWS partner standing, identify the highest-value opportunities, and scope the work before you commit to anything.

How is the Fractional Alliances Leader priced?
+

It's priced based on scope and hours per week, and typically falls between a managed services retainer and a full-time VP of Alliances hire. Exact pricing is confirmed during the scoping call once we understand the program's complexity and your coverage needs.

Do you offer AWS Partner Funding to offset the cost?
+

In many cases, yes. Depending on your partner status and the nature of the engagement, AWS funding programs like MAP, MDF, and POC credits can offset a meaningful portion of our fees. We identify those opportunities during the discovery call.

Who owns the work after the engagement ends?
+

You do. Every playbook, framework, submission document, and process we build is yours to keep and operate independently. We're not in the business of creating dependency.
