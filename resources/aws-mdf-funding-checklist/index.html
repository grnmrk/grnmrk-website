AWS MDF Funding
Checklist

The complete checklist for qualifying, applying, and getting reimbursed for AWS Market Development Funds: what AWS requires at every stage.

Program: Market Development Funds (MDF)
Eligible: Select, Advanced & Premier Partners
Covers: Pre-Approval · Execution · Reimbursement

AWS MDF funds the marketing programs that generate AWS-related pipeline. Most partners lose MDF not because they ran a bad program, but because they did not document it the way AWS requires, or worse, spent budget before getting pre-approval.

This checklist walks through all four stages: partner eligibility, activity pre-approval, execution documentation, and the reimbursement claim. Read the pre-approval section before committing any spend.

MDF Activity Eligibility: Quick Reference
Generally Eligible
Partner-hosted events · AWS-aligned webinars · Digital advertising · Email marketing · Content syndication · Telemarketing · re:Invent / Summit sponsorship · Joint case study production
Typically Not Eligible
Software licenses · Hardware · Employee salaries or travel · Internal training · Generic brand marketing · Activities without pre-approval · Expenses without itemized invoices
