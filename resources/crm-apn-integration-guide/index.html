What Is Inside

Six sections. Everything you need to get connected.

01

Integration Path Comparison

A side-by-side breakdown of every available integration route: the Partner Central API, the AWS Salesforce managed package, Tackle, Suger, Crossbeam, Zapier/Make, and the native Partner Central UI. Includes technical lift, cost, CRM compatibility, and best-fit guidance for each.

02

Account Linking Prerequisites

The exact steps required before any integration can go live: linking your AWS account to Partner Central, accepting ACE Terms and Conditions, IAM role configuration, and the Alliance Lead permissions required to authorize the connection.

03

CRM-to-ACE Field Mapping

A reference table mapping standard CRM opportunity fields to ACE fields in Partner Central. Covers Salesforce and HubSpot natively, with notes on which fields are required for validation, which affect co-sell routing, and which determine funding eligibility.

04

IAM Permissions Setup

The specific AWS IAM policies required for the Partner Central API integration, including AWSPartnerCentralFullAccess, AWSPartnerCentralOpportunityManagement, and AWSMarketplaceSellerOfferManagement. Includes a setup checklist for your IT or DevOps team.

05

ACE Submission Quality Checklist

The 12-point checklist AWS reviewers use to evaluate ACE submissions, translated into actionable items your team can apply to every opportunity before it goes to Partner Central. Covers customer data accuracy, ARR projection requirements, stage definitions, and co-sell request criteria.

06

Integration Decision Framework

A one-page decision tree that helps you choose the right integration path based on your CRM, your pipeline volume, your engineering capacity, and whether AWS Marketplace is part of your go-to-market plan. Built for alliances leaders who need to make a recommendation to their team.
