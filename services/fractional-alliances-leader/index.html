The Case

Fractional vs.
Full-Time Hire.

Both get you senior alliances leadership. Here's what's different.

Grnmrk Fractional Alliances Leader
Fractional
  • Embedded from day one — no ramp time, no recruiting cycle
  • Backed by Grnmrk's full operational infrastructure (managed services, tools, AWS relationships)
  • Costs a fraction of a full-time VP of Alliances salary and benefits package
  • Right-sized to your current program — scales up or down as the partnership matures
  • Deep AWS ecosystem expertise from day one — no internal training required
  • Exit cleanly — no severance, no headcount politics, no performance management
Full-Time VP of Alliances Hire
Full-Time
  • 3–6 month recruiting cycle before anyone is in seat
  • 90-day ramp before they're fully productive — 6+ months to full effectiveness
  • $180–220K+ total comp before benefits, equity, and recruiting costs
  • Fixed headcount cost regardless of where the AWS program actually is
  • Risk of hiring someone without deep AWS program-specific experience
  • Full-time presence and deeper internal integration over the long term

When to make the full-time hire instead

A Fractional Alliances Leader is the right model when you need senior AWS leadership but aren't ready to commit to a full-time hire. When your AWS program reaches the scale where you need someone in-seat five days a week — managing a team, leading cross-functional initiatives, owning board-level relationships — a full-time VP of Alliances is the right answer. We'll tell you when you've reached that point. We're not in the business of staying past our welcome.
