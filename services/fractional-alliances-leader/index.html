Fractional Alliances Leader

Senior AWS alliances leadership. Without the hire.

A Fractional Alliances Leader from Grnmrk is embedded in your business: in your Slack, in your leadership meetings, on your board deck. They own the AWS relationship the way a VP of Alliances would. Not a consultant who hands off a deck. An operator who executes.

This engagement is for companies where the AWS partnership has grown past the point where it runs itself, but a $200K full-time alliances hire is not the right move yet.

Custom scope · Priced between managed services and a full-time hire · Starts with a free scoping call

  • AWS Partnership Strategy
    Strategic

    Sets the annual AWS partnership strategy: tier targets, program investments, Marketplace roadmap, co-sell priority accounts. Presents it to leadership and the board. Not an input into strategy. The owner of it.

  • PDM & AWS Field Relationships
    Relationship

    Owns the PDM relationship directly as the accountable owner, not just a point of contact. Builds relationships with field AEs, PSMs, and specialty org reps in your target territories. You stop wondering whether AWS knows who you are.

  • Co-Sell Motion Leadership
    GTM

    Leads co-sell motion design and execution: ACE pipeline standards, opportunity qualification, AE enablement, and field alignment. Accountable to co-sell pipeline targets, not just activity metrics.

  • Funding & Program Management
    Operations

    Oversees the full AWS funding calendar: MAP, MDF, SaaS Co-sell Benefit, POC credits, SCA. Nothing goes unclaimed. Every eligible deal is submitted correctly. The Grnmrk managed services infrastructure runs the operational layer underneath.

  • Marketplace & Revenue Programs
    Revenue

    Leads Marketplace GTM strategy, private offer program, CPPO channel motion, and partner revenue campaigns, turning the AWS relationship into a measurable pipeline source.

  • Executive & Board Reporting
    Leadership

    Delivers monthly and quarterly AWS program reports to leadership: pipeline attribution, funding captured, tier status, and program ROI. Answers the "what are we getting from this AWS partnership?" question with data.

The Case

Fractional vs. full-time hire.

Both get you senior alliances leadership. Here is what is different.

Grnmrk Fractional Alliances Leader
Fractional
  • Embedded from day one. No ramp time, no recruiting cycle.
  • Backed by Grnmrk's full operational infrastructure, tools, and AWS relationships.
  • Costs a fraction of a full-time VP of Alliances salary and benefits.
  • Right-sized to your current program. Scales as the partnership matures.
  • Deep AWS ecosystem expertise from day one. No internal training needed.
  • Clean exit. No severance, no headcount politics, no performance management.
Full-Time VP of Alliances Hire
Full-Time
  • 3 to 6 month recruiting cycle before anyone is in seat.
  • 90-day ramp before productive. 6+ months to full effectiveness.
  • $180 to 220K total comp before benefits, equity, and recruiting costs.
  • Fixed headcount cost regardless of where the program stands.
  • Risk of hiring someone without deep AWS program-specific experience.
  • Full-time presence and deeper internal integration over the long term.
When to make the full-time hire instead

A Fractional Alliances Leader is right when you need senior AWS leadership but are not ready to commit to a full-time hire. When your program reaches the scale where someone needs to be in seat five days a week, managing a team and owning board-level relationships, a full-time VP of Alliances is the answer. We will tell you when you have reached that point.
