Year one in the partner program sets the trajectory for everything.

Grnmrk gives you a structured approach to year one: clear priorities, active program management, and a bi-weekly cadence that keeps your program on track without requiring a full-time alliances hire.

What a partner manager does

A partner manager is your advocate inside AWS. They explain how programs work, help you navigate partner portal, brief you on funding opportunities, guide your tier advancement, and introduce you to field teams when you are ready to co-sell. Most early-stage partners do not get this. We give it to you.

What's Included

Six areas of support, delivered bi-weekly.

Every area is specific to your partner profile, your tier, and your goals. No generic advice, no templated playbooks.

01
partner portal Setup & Navigation

We confirm your partner path, clean up your company profile, and make sure your solutions are listed correctly. A clean partner portal foundation means nothing blocks you later when you apply for funding, listings, or tier advancement.

02
Tier Roadmap & Requirement Tracking

We map your current standing against your target tier, deliver a written roadmap, and track progress bi-weekly. Clear priorities, clear next actions, no guessing.

03
co-sell Pipeline Setup & First Submissions

We set up your co-sell pipeline correctly, walk you through the submission process, and get your first qualified opportunities in the system. An active co-sell pipeline changes how AWS sees you immediately.

04
Funding Program Awareness & Applications

We identify which MAP, MDF, and other programs you are eligible for and flag application windows in advance. Early-stage partners who know about these programs capture funding their peers miss.

05
Certification Roadmap

We build a certification plan aligned to your tier goals and team size so your people get the right AWS certifications in the right order. Aligned certifications accelerate tier advancement and satisfy AWS competency requirements faster.

06
Bi-weekly Check-in & Progress Review

A dedicated bi-weekly call reviewing tier progress, co-sell pipeline, funding windows, certification status, and any partner portal questions. Consistent cadence keeps everything compounding.
