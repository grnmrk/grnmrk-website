Engagement Stages

  • Launch
    Early-stage foundation

    For teams building a partnerships function for the first time, or restarting one that never settled. We set priorities, ownership, basic workflows, and a cadence so partner work stops living in inboxes and hallway conversations.

  • Lead
    VP-level ownership

    For companies where partnerships need senior ownership without a full-time hire. We own strategy, cross-functional coordination, partner relationships, decision support, and progress reporting the way a VP of Partnerships would.

Scope

What fractional leadership covers.

Consulting work that makes the partnerships function usable across the company. Specialist cloud modules sit under this work when needed.

Core Work
Day to day
  • Program strategy and design: operating model, priorities, ownership, workflows, metrics
  • Relationship and opportunity context for partner work
  • Execution support: briefs, agendas, account plans, QBRs, follow-ups, enablement, updates
  • Workflow guidance: intake, qualification, onboarding, deal registration, co-sell, escalations, renewals
Leadership Layer
How it runs
  • Coordination: objectives to workstreams to owners to dependencies to decisions
  • Decision support when tradeoffs block progress
  • Progress and accountability across the function
  • Shared knowledge quality so the company stays aligned
Specialist modules

Cloud and hyperscaler work (marketplace, funding programs, technical validation) is available as specialist depth under partnership leadership. It is not the category. See Partner Revenue Programs, Cloud Marketplace, and Technical Validation on the services overview.

How We Work

Embedded. Practical. Commercially aware.

We join as owners of the partnerships function, not as a side project. Communication stays short: what changed, why it matters, what should happen next.

    Results
